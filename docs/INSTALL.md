# Invoice Builder Installation Guide

Welcome! This guide will help you install and set up your Invoice Builder app. The whole process takes about 10 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Download the App

1. Open the Google Drive link Sarah sent you
2. Click on **LisaInvoice.exe** to select it
3. Click the **Download** button (down arrow icon at the top)
4. Save it to your **Desktop** so it's easy to find

---

## Step 2: Run the App for the First Time

1. On your Desktop, find the **LisaInvoice.exe** file you just downloaded
2. Double-click it to open

**You'll see a Windows warning screen that says "Windows protected your PC"**

Don't worry - this is normal for new apps. Here's what to do:

3. Click the **"More info"** link (small text on the warning screen)
4. A new button will appear saying **"Run anyway"** - click it
5. The app will now open, and your default web browser will open automatically

**Note:** You only need to do the "Run anyway" step once. After this, the app will open normally.

---

## Step 3: Set Up Gmail App Password

The app needs a special password to send your invoices via email. This is NOT your regular Gmail password - it's a one-time code that's safer.

### Creating Your Gmail App Password:

1. Go to your Google Account settings: **https://myaccount.google.com**
2. Click **Security** in the left menu
3. Scroll down to **"How you sign in to Google"** section
4. Click **"2-Step Verification"** (if not already enabled, follow the prompts to turn it on)
5. Scroll to the bottom and click **"App passwords"**
6. You may need to sign in again
7. Under "Select app," choose **Mail**
8. Under "Select device," choose **Windows Computer**
9. Click **Generate**
10. Google will show you a 16-character password that looks like: **xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx**
11. **Write this down** or keep this window open - you'll need it in the next step

### Important Notes:
- You only need to do this once
- Keep this password private - it's like a key to your email
- If you lose it, you can always generate a new one by repeating these steps

---

## Step 4: Complete Your Profile

When the app opens in your browser, you'll see a home screen with three options.

1. Click **"Edit Profile"** (the bottom option)
2. Fill in or verify all your information:
   - Your full name
   - Your address
   - Your personal email address
   - Your hourly rate (for example: 28.00)
   - Client agency name
   - Client billing email
   - Accountant's email
   - Gmail address (the one you created the app password for)
   - Gmail app password (paste the 16-character code from Step 3)
   - Optional: Add a custom note that will appear on invoices
3. Click **"Save Profile"** at the bottom

The app will show you where your invoices will be saved (usually in your Documents folder).

---

## Step 5: Create a Desktop Shortcut

To make it easy to open the app in the future:

1. Find **LisaInvoice.exe** on your Desktop
2. Right-click on it
3. Choose **"Create shortcut"**
4. A new icon will appear - you can now use this to open the app anytime

**Optional:** You can rename the shortcut to just "Invoices" if you prefer:
- Right-click the shortcut → **Rename** → Type "Invoices" → Press Enter

---

## You're All Set!

The app is now installed and ready to use. Here's what you can do:

- **Weekly Invoice** - Create and send your weekly invoices
- **Monthly Report** - Generate monthly summaries for your accountant
- **Edit Profile** - Update your information anytime

### Tips:
- Double-click your Desktop shortcut anytime to open the app
- The app saves all your invoices automatically to your Documents folder
- You'll see a preview before sending any invoice or report
